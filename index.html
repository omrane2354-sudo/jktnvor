Report It Once. We'll Fix the Rest.

Upload a photo + location — our AI identifies civic issues like potholes, garbage, and broken streetlights, then groups duplicate reports automatically.

The Problem

Citizens report potholes, garbage, broken streetlights, and water leaks — but complaints are often duplicated and difficult to prioritize.

Our Solution

Upload a photo + location. Our AI identifies the problem, estimates severity, and categorizes it automatically.

Special Feature

Multiple reports of the same issue are automatically grouped together — no more duplicate complaints.

Example

8 people report the same pothole → CivicFix combines them into one high-priority issue with 8 confirmations.
